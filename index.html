35 Smith Street, Mooloolaba

A boutique building of seventeen homes.

Lucia is a six-storey building on Smith Street with only seventeen apartments. Fifteen are two-bedroom residences, and two are three-bedroom penthouses on the top floor.

Curved balconies, garden planters and patterned timber screens give every home its own outdoor room, with natural stone at street level.
