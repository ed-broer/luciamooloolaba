Introducing Lucia

Close to everything. Tucked away from it all.

Lucia, Mooloolaba. A rare chance to secure a home in a quiet cul-de-sac, just a two-minute walk from the Esplanade. Comprising just seventeen residences, each designed to capture natural light and coastal breezes, with a calm, considered character that feels right at home by the sea.

Registered buyers get first access, a week before public release.

The lifestyle

The Mooloolaba life

Start the morning with a swim between the flags, then a coffee on the Esplanade. Walk over Alex Hill, or spend the afternoon at the Spit with fish and chips. Meet friends for sunset drinks at Avani, then wander down to The Wharf for dinner. All without touching your car keys.
